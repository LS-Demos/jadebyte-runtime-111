FAQ

Common questions

Who is Girish Lade?
I'm an independent full-stack developer, UI/UX designer and product builder based in Pune, Maharashtra, India. I'm also the founder of LadeStack, an ecosystem of practical digital tools.
What is LadeStack?
LadeStack brings together useful browser-first applications, developer utilities, productivity tools and AI-assisted workflows. The focus is on accessible, practical and thoughtfully designed software.
Is GB Coder free to use?
GB Coder is currently free to use. Its AI features follow a bring-your-own-key model, so any external AI provider costs depend on the user's own API account. The goal is to keep the core development experience free.
What is your educational background?
My engineering background is in Mechanical Engineering. My interests expanded into software development, interface design, browser-native computing and product development through self-directed learning.
What technologies do you work with?
My work spans modern web development, frontend and backend workflows, UI/UX design, Git-based development, browser-native computing and AI-assisted product development.
Where can I explore your work or contact you?
Explore my public projects through GitHub and the official LadeStack website. You can also connect with me on LinkedIn or send an email to admin@ladestack.in.
